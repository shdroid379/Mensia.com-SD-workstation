The Mensia Guide

How each research mode works, how to get sharper answers, and the questions people ask most about AI search engines.

The three research modes
Casual Latency optimised

Pure conversational reasoning with no external web calls — best for brainstorming, quick explanations, and drafting where speed matters more than fresh data.

Search Verified grounding

Queries live web indexes and runs a secondary audit pass to eliminate hallucinations — the strong default for current-events and fact-based inquiries.

Deep Research Multi-source synthesis

Fires several concurrent queries across independent sources and synthesises them into one audited report — ideal for market analysis and technical research.

Prompting for sharper answers
01
Name a timeframe

Add "as of 2026" or "this quarter" so Search and Deep Research prioritise the freshest sources over older indexed pages.

02
Ask for a stance

"Compare X vs Y for a small team" gets a sharper, more decision-ready answer than an open-ended question with no constraints.

03
Verify the citations

Every Search and Deep Research answer cites its sources inline — click any numbered badge to open the source page and verify directly.
