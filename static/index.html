Why Mensia

Not another guessing chatbot.

Most AI chat tools are optimized to sound confident, not to be correct — they fill gaps in memory with plausible-sounding text.

Mensia runs a dual-model cognitive audit: it searches the live web, cross-checks claims across multiple independent sources, and forces a second internal pass to review its own reasoning before the answer ever reaches you.

The four research modes

Pick the mode that matches how much verification the question deserves.

Casual

Pure conversational reasoning with no external web calls — best for brainstorming, quick explanations, and drafting where speed matters more than fresh data.

Latency optimized

Search

Queries live web indexes and runs a secondary audit pass to reduce hallucinations — a strong default for current-events and fact-based questions.

Verified grounding

Deep Research

Runs several concurrent queries across independent sources and synthesizes them into one audited report — ideal for comparisons and market or technical research.

Multi-source synthesis

Intense Dive

An unlockable, exhaustive weekly deep-dive with optional academic paper sourcing — reserved for your single most important question of the week.
